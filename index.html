About Me

I am a researcher working at the intersection of logistics, supply chain management, operations and data analytics. My work focuses particularly on data-driven decision making, demand forecasting, transportation, emerging technologies and smart supply chains.

My academic work combines quantitative and computational approaches with managerial research. This allows me to investigate not only whether an analytical or technological solution works, but also how it can be implemented within organisations and complex supply-chain environments.

Before joining KFUPM, I served as an Assistant Professor in Pakistan and have experience spanning university teaching, research supervision, academic administration and industry operations.

Research

My research agenda connects analytical methods with practical problems in logistics, transportation and operations.

Supply Chain & Logistics

Supply-chain resilience, last-mile logistics, operations management, transportation and digitally enabled logistics systems.

AI & Business Analytics

Machine learning, predictive analytics, decision-support systems and data-driven approaches to operational decision making.

Demand Forecasting

Forecasting of intermittent, volatile and disaggregated demand using statistical and machine-learning approaches.

Smart Mobility

Transportation systems, mobility analytics, last-mile delivery and data-driven approaches to sustainable mobility.

Emerging Technologies

Blockchain, digital transformation, technology adoption and the role of emerging technologies in supply-chain management.

Operations Research

Quantitative modelling, simulation, optimisation and decision-support approaches for complex operational problems.
